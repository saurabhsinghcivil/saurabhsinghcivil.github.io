About Me

A safety professional who makes workplaces safer — at scale

I'm Saurabh Singh, an EHS Manager with over a decade of experience safeguarding workers on some of India's and South America's most demanding infrastructure projects — from nuclear power plants to a 1,500 km HVDC transmission line cutting across Chile.

My career spans Kalpataru Projects International, Larsen & Toubro, BHEL, and NPCIL — organisations where safety isn't a checkbox but a discipline. I hold an M.Tech in Safety & Occupational Health Engineering from IIEST Shibpur and a B.Tech in Civil Engineering, giving me both the technical depth to understand construction processes and the specialist knowledge to protect the people executing them.

I believe that a well-designed safety culture reduces incidents not by restricting work, but by enabling it — smarter, faster, and sustainably. That conviction has helped me cut workplace accidents by 30% and safety-related complaints by 50% in past roles.
